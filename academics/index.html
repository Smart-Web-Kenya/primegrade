Pathways

Our educational structure

The Prime Grade School offers Early Years, Primary School and Junior School pathways, with boarding for selected grades.

Early Years (Pre-School)

Playgroup, PP1 and PP2

PlaygroupPP1PP2

Our Pre-School pathway gives young learners a caring start, building confidence, curiosity and readiness for Primary School through engaging teaching and individual attention.

  • Caring, age-appropriate teaching and guidance
  • Hands-on projects that bring early learning to life
  • Foundation for literacy, communication and social growth

Primary School

Grade 1 to Grade 6

Grades 1–6Boarding for Grades 4–6

Primary School builds a strong academic foundation in literacy, numeracy, scientific understanding and critical thinking, alongside character, talent development and practical life skills.

  • Strong academic foundation across core learning areas
  • Access to library, IT room and equipped laboratory
  • Boarding available for Grades 4 to 6, with structured study time and closer academic support
  • Arts, sport, clubs and hands-on learning as part of school life

Junior School

Grade 7 to Grade 9

Grades 7–9Boarding available

Junior School continues academic progress with greater independence, digital literacy, confident communication and leadership through teamwork and service — preparing learners for the next stage of education.

  • Continued academic growth with individual care
  • Digital literacy through a fully functional computer room
  • Hands-on science learning in an equipped laboratory
  • Boarding available with modern ablution block, kitchen, dining hall and dormitories
